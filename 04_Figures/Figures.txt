# Figures

This directory contains all graphical outputs generated during the Density Functional Theory (DFT) and Time-Dependent Density Functional Theory (TD-DFT) investigation of Curcumin_3OH using ORCA.

## Figure Descriptions

### optimized_structure.png

Optimized molecular geometry of Curcumin_3OH obtained after DFT geometry optimization. The structure corresponds to a local minimum on the potential energy surface and was used for all subsequent calculations.

### HOMO.png

Visualization of the Highest Occupied Molecular Orbital (HOMO), illustrating the electron-rich regions of the molecule and their contribution to electronic transitions.

### LUMO.png

Visualization of the Lowest Unoccupied Molecular Orbital (LUMO), highlighting the electron-accepting regions involved in excitation processes.

### Energy_Gap.png

Frontier molecular orbital energy diagram showing the HOMO energy, LUMO energy, and the calculated HOMO–LUMO energy gap (0.420 eV). The energy gap provides insight into the electronic reactivity and charge-transfer characteristics of the molecule.

### UV_Vis.png

TD-DFT simulated UV–Visible absorption spectrum of Curcumin_3OH. The strongest electronic transition was observed at λmax = 304.8 nm with an oscillator strength of f = 0.936.

## Scientific Significance

The figures presented in this directory provide visual insight into the structural stability, electronic properties, frontier orbital characteristics, and optical behavior of Curcumin_3OH. Together, they support the interpretation of molecular reactivity and excited-state properties obtained from the quantum chemical calculations.
