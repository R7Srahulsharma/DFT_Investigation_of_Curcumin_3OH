# Frequency Analysis

## Objective

To verify that the optimized geometry corresponds to a true minimum on the potential energy surface.

## Computational Method

Frequency calculations were performed at the optimized DFT geometry.

## Results

No imaginary frequencies were observed.

Therefore, the optimized structure represents a true local minimum.

## Significance

The absence of imaginary frequencies confirms the stability of the optimized molecular geometry and validates the use of the structure for further electronic property calculations.
