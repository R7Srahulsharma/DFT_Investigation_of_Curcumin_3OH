# HOMO–LUMO Analysis

## Objective

To investigate the frontier molecular orbitals and electronic properties of Curcumin_3OH.

## Computational Method

- Software: ORCA
- Functional: B3LYP-D3BJ
- Basis Set: def2-TZVP

## Frontier Orbital Energies

| Orbital | Energy (eV) |
|----------|-------------|
| HOMO | -2.079 |
| LUMO | -1.659 |
| HOMO–LUMO Gap | 0.420 |

## Results

The HOMO–LUMO energy gap was calculated to be 0.420 eV.

A relatively small frontier orbital energy gap suggests:

- Enhanced electronic softness
- Improved charge-transfer capability
- Increased chemical reactivity
- Favorable electronic excitation behavior

## Orbital Distribution

The HOMO is primarily localized over the conjugated aromatic framework, indicating electron-rich regions.

The LUMO exhibits delocalization across the molecular backbone, suggesting efficient electron acceptance upon excitation.
