# Global Reactivity Descriptors

## Objective

To evaluate the chemical reactivity and stability of Curcumin_3OH using conceptual DFT descriptors.

## Koopmans-Based Equations

IP = -EHOMO

EA = -ELUMO

η = (IP - EA)/2

S = 1/(2η)

χ = (IP + EA)/2

ω = χ²/(2η)

## Calculated Values

| Descriptor | Value |
|------------|---------|
| Ionization Potential (IP) | 2.079 eV |
| Electron Affinity (EA) | 1.659 eV |
| Chemical Hardness (η) | 0.210 eV |
| Softness (S) | 2.381 eV⁻¹ |
| Electronegativity (χ) | 1.869 eV |
| Electrophilicity Index (ω) | 8.319 eV |

## Discussion

The relatively low hardness and high softness indicate enhanced polarizability and chemical responsiveness.

The electrophilicity index suggests a significant tendency to accept electronic charge during intermolecular interactions.

The calculated descriptors support the observed electronic excitation behavior and frontier orbital characteristics.

## Conclusion

Global reactivity descriptors indicate that Curcumin_3OH possesses favorable charge-transfer capability, moderate stability, and enhanced electronic responsiveness.
