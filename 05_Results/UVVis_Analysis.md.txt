# TD-DFT UV–Vis Analysis

## Objective

To investigate the electronic excitation behavior and optical properties of Curcumin_3OH.

## Computational Method

- Software: ORCA
- Method: TD-DFT/TDA
- Functional: B3LYP-D3BJ
- Basis Set: def2-SVP

## Major Electronic Transitions

| Excited State | Wavelength (nm) | Oscillator Strength (f) |
|--------------|----------------|-------------------------|
| S1 | 304.8 | 0.936 |
| S2 | 289.6 | 0.188 |
| S3 | 282.7 | 0.099 |
| S4 | 275.6 | 0.379 |

## Key Result

The strongest absorption band was observed at:

- λmax = 304.8 nm
- Oscillator Strength (f) = 0.936

## Interpretation

The high oscillator strength indicates a strongly allowed electronic transition.

The absorption band arises from excitation involving frontier molecular orbitals and reflects the extended π-conjugation present in Curcumin_3OH.
