# Results Summary

## Project Overview

This study presents a Density Functional Theory (DFT) and Time-Dependent Density Functional Theory (TD-DFT) investigation of Curcumin_3OH using the ORCA quantum chemistry package.

## Calculations Performed

- Geometry Optimization
- Frequency Analysis
- Single Point Energy Calculation
- HOMO–LUMO Analysis
- Global Reactivity Descriptor Calculation
- TD-DFT UV–Vis Analysis

## Key Findings

| Property | Value |
|-----------|-----------|
| HOMO Energy | -2.079 eV |
| LUMO Energy | -1.659 eV |
| HOMO–LUMO Gap | 0.420 eV |
| λmax | 304.8 nm |
| Oscillator Strength (f) | 0.936 |

## Conclusions

The calculated HOMO–LUMO gap indicates enhanced electronic softness and charge-transfer capability. TD-DFT calculations revealed an intense absorption band at 304.8 nm, demonstrating favorable electronic excitation characteristics of Curcumin_3OH.
