# Computational Methodology

## Quantum Chemical Calculations

All quantum chemical calculations were carried out using the ORCA 6.0 program package.

---

## Geometry Optimization

Method:

- DFT
- B3LYP-D3BJ functional
- def2-SVP basis set

Purpose:

To obtain the minimum-energy molecular structure.

---

## Vibrational Frequency Analysis

Performed at the same level of theory as geometry optimization.

Purpose:

- Confirm optimized structure
- Verify absence of imaginary frequencies

---

## Single Point Energy Calculation

Method:

- B3LYP-D3BJ
- def2-TZVP

Purpose:

Improve electronic energy accuracy.

---

## Frontier Molecular Orbital Analysis

HOMO and LUMO orbitals were calculated and visualized using cube files generated by ORCA.

Purpose:

- Electronic structure characterization
- Energy gap determination

---

## Global Reactivity Descriptor Analysis

Descriptors were calculated using Koopmans' approximation:

IP = -EHOMO

EA = -ELUMO

η = (IP − EA)/2

S = 1/(2η)

χ = (IP + EA)/2

ω = χ²/(2η)

---

## TD-DFT Calculations

Method:

- TD-DFT/TDA
- B3LYP-D3BJ
- def2-SVP

Purpose:

Prediction of electronic absorption spectrum.

---

## Visualization

Software:

- Avogadro
- OriginPro

Used for:

- Molecular structure rendering
- Orbital visualization
- UV-Vis spectral plotting

---
