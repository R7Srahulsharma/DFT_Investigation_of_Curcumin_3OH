# DFT and TD-DFT Investigation of Curcumin_3OH

## Abstract

A comprehensive Density Functional Theory (DFT) and Time-Dependent Density Functional Theory (TD-DFT) investigation was performed on Curcumin_3OH using the ORCA quantum chemistry package. Structural optimization, vibrational frequency analysis, frontier molecular orbital analysis, global reactivity descriptor calculations, and UV-Visible spectral simulations were conducted to evaluate the molecular stability, electronic properties, and optical behavior of the compound.

---

## Objectives

- Optimize molecular geometry
- Verify structural stability
- Analyze electronic properties
- Determine HOMO-LUMO energy gap
- Evaluate global reactivity descriptors
- Simulate UV-Vis absorption spectrum

---

## Main Findings

### HOMO Energy

-2.079 eV

### LUMO Energy

-1.659 eV

### HOMO-LUMO Gap

0.420 eV

### Maximum Absorption

304.8 nm

### Oscillator Strength

0.936

---

## Scientific Significance

The relatively small HOMO-LUMO gap indicates enhanced electronic responsiveness and efficient charge-transfer capability.

The TD-DFT calculations predict a strong UV absorption band, demonstrating significant optical activity.

---

## Conclusion

The DFT and TD-DFT results reveal that Curcumin_3OH exhibits favorable electronic and optical characteristics, making it an interesting candidate for further theoretical and experimental investigations.
